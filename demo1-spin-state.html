Why isn't one Bloch sphere enough?

A qubit has 2 free real numbers after normalization and global phase — the sphere's surface holds it losslessly. A qutrit pure state has 4; a mixed one has 8 (the SU(3) Bloch vector lives in 8 dimensions). You can't fit that on 18 spheres — or any number. The faithful object is 8-dimensional.

So the matrix on the right isn't decoration — it's the source of truth. The sphere on the left is an honest shadow of it: the two-level slice you drive with microwaves. Watch the off-diagonal cells light up with hue as you add transverse field or strain — that phase is exactly what the single sphere throws away.
