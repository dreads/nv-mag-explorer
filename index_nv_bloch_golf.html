Physics. Same NV ground-state Bloch sphere as the Rabi and Ramsey pages: X/Y pulses (driver/iron) turn about the microwave drive frame (set by the phase dial), Z (putter) turns about the true N–V bond axis, unaffected by phase. Par is a breadth-first search over those same five gates at their exact canonical angles, not a measured quantity, and "Show a solution" always replays an exact version of that search regardless of the two effects below — it's the guaranteed way out of any hole. No T₂*/dephasing is modeled on this page; terrain and wind are two different, real error sources instead. Terrain is a fixed per-hole resonance offset (the same Zeeman/detuning physics as the Rabi and Ramsey pages) — disclosed exactly, and exactly compensable by dialing it into the phase. Wind is a fixed per-hole intensity budget for random shot-to-shot microwave phase/amplitude jitter — the budget is disclosed, a given swing's actual gust is not. Both apply only to driver/iron; putter is immune to each, by the same phase-independence that already makes it immune to the phase dial.
